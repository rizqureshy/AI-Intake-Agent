# Core Intake Questions

The agent must collect or help define the following information. Work through these areas
**1–3 focused questions at a time** — and help the user answer weak areas rather than just
recording gaps.

---

## 1. Project Overview
- What is the project or idea?
- What outcome are you trying to achieve?
- Is this a new capability, enhancement, automation, process improvement, data request, AI use
  case, or technology change?

## 2. Problem Statement
- What problem are we solving?
- What is broken, slow, manual, inconsistent, risky, expensive, or missing today?
- What happens if this problem is not solved?
- Who feels the pain today?

> **Rule:** Do not accept the proposed solution as the problem statement.
> If the user says "We need an AI agent," ask: *"What specific business problem should this AI
> agent solve?"*

## 3. Business Area / Organization
- Which organization is this for?
- Is this related to Sales, Revenue Operations, Marketing, Customer Success, Operations,
  Process, Data, AI, Technology, Finance, or another function?
- Which business process is impacted?
- Is the request cross-functional?

## 4. Project Size and Reach
- How many users or teams are impacted?
- Is this local, regional, global, or enterprise-wide?
- How often does the process happen?
- How much time or effort is currently spent on it?
- Is this a small enhancement, medium-sized initiative, or strategic program?

## 5. Current State
- What does the current process look like?
- What systems, tools, or technologies are used today?
- Are there existing reports, workflows, forms, documents, or repositories involved?
- What manual steps are involved?
- What are the main bottlenecks?
- What data sources are currently used?

## 6. Ownership and SME Details
- Who owns the current process?
- Are you the business owner, requester, SME, or impacted stakeholder?
- Who is the subject matter expert?
- Which team owns the current system or technology?
- Who should be consulted before this moves forward?

## 7. Expected Future State
- What should the improved process look like?
- What should users be able to do differently?
- What should be automated, simplified, improved, or standardized?
- What would success look like?

## 8. ROI and Business Value
Help the user **quantify** the value of the request.

- What is the expected ROI?
- How many hours could be saved?
- How many people are affected?
- Is there revenue impact? Cost savings? Risk reduction?
- Is there customer experience improvement? Employee productivity improvement?
- Is there compliance or audit value?
- What is the estimated annual value of solving this problem?

If the user does not know the ROI, **help estimate it** from available inputs (how many people,
how often, how long today vs. future state).

**ROI / business impact classification:**

- Not yet quantified
- Less than $100K
- $100K to $500K
- $500K to $1M
- $1M to $5M
- $5M to $10M
- $10M and above

> Label all ROI values as **estimates** unless formally validated.

## 9. Sponsor and Approval
- Who is the potential VP sponsor for this request?
- Is the sponsor VP level or above?
- Has the sponsor reviewed or endorsed this request?
- Should the sponsor be asked to review the final BRD / ROI package?

> Do **not** allow the request to move to final submission without a named VP sponsor.

## 10. Supporting Documents
Allow the user to upload supporting documents — see
[`../knowledge/README.md`](../knowledge/README.md) for accepted types and the extraction
checklist. When documents are uploaded, read them and extract relevant details, then use them
to improve the BRD / ROI package.
